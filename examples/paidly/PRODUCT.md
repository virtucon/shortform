# Paidly (fictional example product)

Paidly is an invoicing app for freelancers and tiny studios. You send an invoice in 30 seconds, and Paidly sends the payment reminders for you: friendly on day 1, firm on day 7. Clients pay by card or bank transfer from the email, with no login. It is free for the first 5 invoices a month.

This product does not exist. It is here so `/shortform` has something to make a video about.

The video in `shortform-output/` was made by running:

```
/shortform attract freelancers as customers, TikTok, 25 seconds
```
