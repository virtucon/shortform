# Objective archetypes

Map the user's objective to the nearest archetype. The archetype sets the hook style, the scene structure and the call to action. The user's own words always override a default here; an archetype is a starting shape.

If the objective sits between two archetypes, pick the one that matches the **action** the viewer should take, and borrow hook style from the other.

| Archetype | Viewer should | Hook style | Default CTA |
|---|---|---|---|
| `attract-customers` | want the product | their pain, stated bluntly | "Try it free — link in bio" |
| `drive-signups` | join a waitlist, trial or newsletter | outcome plus scarcity or timing | "Join the waitlist — link in bio" |
| `grow-followers` | follow the account | curiosity gap, promise of a series | "Follow for part 2" |
| `educate` | learn one thing and save the video | a counter-intuitive claim or a "how to" | "Save this for later" |
| `announce-launch` | know it exists and look | the reveal: "We just shipped…" | "Out now — link in bio" |
| `social-proof` | trust it | a real result or quote first | "See why — link in bio" |
| `hire` | apply or share with someone who would | the mission or the unusual perk | "We're hiring — link in bio" |
| `community` | star, contribute or join | the problem devs share | "Star it on GitHub" |

## Structures

Timings are for a 25-second video. Scale proportionally, keeping the hook at 2–3s and the CTA at 3–4s. For 15 seconds, drop to two middle beats.

### attract-customers
`Pain hook (0–2.5s)` → `Agitate: what it costs them (2.5–6s)` → `Reveal the product (6–9s)` → `Three proof beats: real UI doing the job (9–21s)` → `CTA (21–25s)`

### drive-signups
`Outcome hook (0–2.5s)` → `What it is, one line (2.5–6s)` → `Two beats of what they get (6–15s)` → `Why now: launch date, limited spots, early price — only if true (15–21s)` → `CTA (21–25s)`

### grow-followers
`Curiosity hook (0–2.5s)` → `Deliver real value fast: 3 quick beats (2.5–18s)` → `Tease what comes next (18–21s)` → `Follow CTA (21–25s)`
Never withhold the promised payoff; tease the *next* one.

### educate
`Claim or question hook (0–2.5s)` → `Step 1 / Step 2 / Step 3, each numbered on screen (2.5–20s)` → `One-line recap (20–22s)` → `Save CTA (22–25s)`
A visible progress marker ("1/3") holds retention.

### announce-launch
`Reveal hook (0–2.5s)` → `What it does in one line (2.5–6s)` → `Two or three highlight beats (6–20s)` → `CTA with where to get it (20–25s)`

### social-proof
`Result or quote hook (0–3s)` → `Who said or achieved it (3–6s)` → `How the product got them there: 2 beats (6–18s)` → `Second proof point (18–21s)` → `CTA (21–25s)`
Only real, sourced proof. If the source has none, switch to `attract-customers`.

### hire
`Mission or perk hook (0–2.5s)` → `What the team builds (2.5–8s)` → `What the role does (8–15s)` → `Why it is a good place: 2 concrete facts (15–21s)` → `CTA (21–25s)`

### community
`Shared-problem hook (0–2.5s)` → `The tool, shown working (2.5–10s)` → `Two reasons it is worth a star: speed, size, licence, numbers (10–20s)` → `CTA with repo name on screen (20–25s)`

## Hook patterns

Write three hooks from three different patterns. Each is at most 8 words on screen, readable in under 2 seconds, and true.

| Pattern | Shape | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Pain | name the viewer's problem | "Still chasing invoices by email?" |
| Outcome | the after state, with a number | "Invoices paid in 2 days, not 30" |
| Contrarian | reject a common belief | "You don't need an accountant for this" |
| Curiosity gap | promise a specific reveal | "The 1 setting that halved our build time" |
| Call-out | name the audience | "Freelancers: stop doing this" |
| Reveal | show the thing at once | "We built Stripe for plumbers" |
| Proof | lead with a result | "4,000 devs starred this in a week" |

Rank the three. Best first, with one line on why.

## Call to action rules

- One CTA. Never two.
- Short-form platforms do not make caption links clickable for most accounts. Say "link in bio", show a short URL or handle on screen as text, or ask for an in-app action (follow, save, comment a keyword).
- Match the ask to the objective: a follower video asks for a follow, not a purchase.
- Put the CTA text in the vertical middle of the safe zone, hold it at least 2.5 seconds, and keep the brand name or handle visible with it.
